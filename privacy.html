Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy explains what information the MetaZone desktop application (“MetaZone”, developed by HASIBNIKON) and its account service collect, why, how it is stored and shared, and how to have it deleted. It applies to MetaZone’s use of “Sign in with Google”.

Google user data

What we access. MetaZone requests only the basic Google Sign-In scopes openid, email and profile. Through them we receive your Google account’s email address, name and profile picture address. We do not request, access or receive your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service data.

How we use it. Only to identify your MetaZone account, apply your plan (Free or Premium) and its limits, show your email address inside the app, and let the administrator find your account to activate or renew Premium. We use your email address for these purposes only; we do not use your name or picture for anything.

How we store it. Your email address is stored in our account database hosted by Supabase. Your name and picture address may be kept in the sign-in record created by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) but are not otherwise used. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by access controls so that each signed-in user can reach only their own account information. On your PC, MetaZone keeps its sign-in session in Windows protected storage.

How we share it. We do not sell, rent or share Google user data. It is not used for advertising, and it is not transferred to anyone except our infrastructure providers (Supabase, and Google itself for sign-in) acting on our behalf, or where required by law.

Retention and deletion. We keep your account information while your account exists. To delete your account and the data associated with it, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address you signed in with; we will process the request within 30 days. You can also withdraw MetaZone’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use. MetaZone’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in information. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address and the basic profile information Google provides through Sign-In (such as your name and profile picture address). We use your email address to identify your account. We do not request or receive access to any other Google data or services.
  • Account status. Your plan (Free or Premium), Premium start and expiry dates, and whether the account is active or suspended. The administrator may add a short note when activating Premium (for example a payment reference).
  • Usage counts. How many generations you ran per day, whether each succeeded or failed, and lightweight event records: time, plan, kind of generation, AI provider and model name, and app version. Event records are kept for about 45 days; daily totals are kept while your account exists.
  • Activity. The time MetaZone last contacted our service and the app version you use (a periodic “heartbeat”).

What we do NOT collect

  • Your images, generated titles, descriptions, keywords or prompts — these stay on your computer.
  • Your AI provider API keys — they are stored on your computer and sent only from your computer to the AI provider you chose.
  • Your Google password. Sign-in happens in your web browser on Google’s own page.

How we use it

To sign you in, apply your plan and its limits, activate and expire Premium, show you your usage, keep the service secure, and understand overall usage and app versions. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with our service provider Supabase. Sign-in is provided by Google. Data is protected by access controls so that each user can only reach their own account information through the app.

Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except the service providers above, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To request access to, or deletion of, your account and associated data, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address you signed in with. Deleting your account removes your usage records and subscription history.

Children

MetaZone is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
