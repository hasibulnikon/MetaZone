MetaZone

AI-powered stock image metadata generation and batch metadata embedding for Windows.

MetaZone is a free-to-try Windows desktop application for stock media contributors. It generates titles, descriptions and keywords for your images using the AI provider accounts (API keys) that you configure, and embeds the metadata into your files using ExifTool. Your images and your API keys stay on your computer.

Plans

  • Free — sign in with Google; limited daily and weekly generations; one API key per provider.
  • Premium — unlimited generations and multiple API keys per provider, activated on your Google account by the administrator for 30 days at a time.

Sign-in

MetaZone uses “Sign in with Google” only to identify your account (your email address). It requests no access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.
